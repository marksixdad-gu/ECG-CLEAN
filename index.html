A Compact Floor-Cleaning Option for Smaller Spaces

Designed for areas where a larger floor machine may be inconvenient, QY-01 combines spraying, scrubbing, suction and squeegee functions in a compact cordless format.

  • Compact format for smaller indoor hard-floor areas
  • Cordless operation with a replaceable battery
  • Spray, scrub, suction and water recovery functions
  • Potential applications include restaurants, hotels, healthcare and other facilities, subject to site requirements
